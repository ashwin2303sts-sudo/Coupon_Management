Coupon Calculation & Lifecycle Logic Guide

MATH & BASE RULES

Lifetime Earned

Booking Fare × Rule %

Total coupon rewards accrued across all bookings based on the matched Rule % (Office ID, Booking Type, Airline, Fare Type).

Pending Maturity

Locked: Travel Date + 1 Day

Coupon balance locked safely during travel duration until 1 day post-travel to prevent cancellation leakage.

Available Coupons

Auto-Credit post Travel

Automatically transferred from Pending to Available wallet upon travel completion (+1 day), ready for instant redemption.

Total Redeemed

Payable = Fare - Coupon

Total coupon credit utilized to discount net passenger fares on subsequent bookings.

Coupon Lifecycle: 1. Booking → Fare × Rule % (Pending) → 2. Travel + 1 Day → Auto-Credit (Available) → 3. Next Booking → Redeemed (Discount)
